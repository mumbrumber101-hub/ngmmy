Drag to turn the scene. Pinch or use the zoom buttons to move closer. Select a world or a connecting line to read its sources. Use Find in this scene or List to choose an entry by name.

Keyboard: focus the map, then use the arrow keys to turn, + or − to zoom, Home to reset, and [ or ] to move between places. Enter opens the selected place in the reading panel.

The Scene menu offers five views: Butler’s catalogue, the island-planets of Selkirk, the voyage of Space Chantey, the enclosed worlds, and Aeaea’s shared underground. In the catalogue, lines join each world to its sun and each moon to its world. In the voyage, arrows follow the order of the narrative; the dashed arrow marks the return through reversed time, and the dotted arrow Roadstrum’s disputed report of a last leg by way of Mars. In the enclosed worlds, frames show the model in Barnaby’s study with its city, and the realm inside Toby’s bullet. Sizes and positions are schematic.
